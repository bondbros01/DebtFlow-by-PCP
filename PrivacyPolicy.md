Privacy Policy for DebtFlow
Effective date: September 6, 2026
Problem Child Performance ("us", "we", or "our") operates the DebtFlow mobile application (the "Service"). This app functions strictly as a manual debt and payment tracker.
This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

   1. No Banking or Financial Transactions
   DebtFlow is entirely a manual tracking and planning utility.


* The Service does not connect to bank accounts, credit cards, or financial institutions.
* The Service does not digitally move money, process payments, or execute financial transactions.
* Any financial information, debt amounts, or payment histories you input are handled locally on your device or managed strictly according to the terms below.


   1. Information Collection and Use
   We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

Types of Data Collected:

* User-Inputted Financial Tracking Data: Any data you enter manually regarding your debts, balances, and payment schedules is stored locally on your device unless otherwise backed up by your explicit choice.
* Personal Data: While using our Service, we do not require you to provide personally identifiable information unless you explicitly choose to create an account or contact support.
* Usage Data: We may collect information that your mobile device sends whenever you use our Service. This may include your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g., IP address), device type, operating system version, and general diagnostic data to ensure app performance.


   1. Use of Data
   DebtFlow uses the collected data for various purposes:


* To provide and maintain the Service
* To monitor app performance and stability
* To provide customer care and support
* To detect, prevent, and address technical issues


   1. Service Providers
   We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service, perform app-related services (such as crash reporting or analytics), or assist us in analyzing how our Service is used. These third parties have access to your data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.
   2. Security
   The security of your data is important to us. Because DebtFlow does not store financial credentials or move real money, the primary security boundary exists on your physical device. We recommend using device-level security locks (biometrics or PINs) to safeguard your manually entered data.
   3. Children's Privacy
   Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13 ("Children"). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
   4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
   We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective date" at the top.
